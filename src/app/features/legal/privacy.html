This page explains what information {{ platform.info().platform_name }} collects about you, why, who can see it and what you can ask us to do with it. We keep it short and plain on purpose.

1. What we collect

InformationWhy we need it
Your name, username, email address and password (stored only in a scrambled form that we cannot read)To create your account and let you sign in.
Your phone number, if you give itSo we can reach you or confirm who you are when you ask for help.

Technical records such as sign-in time, your IP address and error reportsTo keep the service safe and working, and to stop misuse.

We do not ask for payment card, bank or government ID details, and we do not run advertising.

2. How it is used

  • To run the service you asked for: tests, results, ranks, study plans, reminders and messages from us in the bell.
  • To help your teachers or the administrators of the service see how you and your batch are doing, so they can help you. Teachers and administrators can see your name, contact details, batches and results.
  • To fix mistakes in questions and improve the service. When we look at results across many students, we use totals and averages.

We do not sell your information.

3. Who else can see it

  • Other students can see your name only on a leaderboard, if the test shows one. They cannot see your results, contact details or answers.
  • Service providers who host the site for us (for example a web host, a database provider and a place that stores pictures). They handle the data on our behalf and only to run the service.
  • The law. We may share information if the law or a court requires it.

4. What stays on your device

The service keeps a sign-in token, your light or dark choice and your language in your browser, so you stay signed in and the pages look the way you chose. It does not use these for advertising. Clearing your browser data or signing out removes them.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your account and your results while your account is open, so your history and progress stay available. If you ask us to delete your account, we remove your personal details and your account. Some records may stay in backups for a short time, and we may keep totals that no longer identify you.

6. Your choices

  • Change your name, phone, goal and language, and turn the weekly summary on or off, from your profile.
  • Ask us to show you the information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete your account.
  • Ask us to stop messages that are not needed to run your account.

Contact us using the details below. We will check it is really you before we act on a request.

7. Keeping it safe

Connections to the service are encrypted, passwords are stored in a scrambled form, and access to other people's information is limited to the people who need it. No service can be perfectly safe, so please choose a strong password and keep it private.

8. Children

The service is meant for people preparing for exams. If you are under 18, please use it with the knowledge of a parent or guardian. A parent or guardian can contact us to see or delete a child's information.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the last change, and we will tell you in the service if the change matters. Our terms of use explain the rules for using the service.
